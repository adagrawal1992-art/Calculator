Black screen (“phone off”) — secret inputs, no feedback shown:
• Moment mode: tap LEFT half = +1 hour. Tap RIGHT-TOP quarter = +10 minutes. Tap RIGHT-BOTTOM quarter = +1 minute. Example 4:37 → 4 left, 3 right-top, 7 right-bottom.
• Minutes mode: each tap anywhere = +1 minute ahead. No taps = default N.
• Swipe DOWN on the black screen = clear what you entered.
• Touch & hold ½ second = wake the lock screen.

On the lock screen:
• Tap the big clock = time rolls minute by minute back to the real time.
• Touch & hold anywhere except the clock for 1 second = back to black (“phone off”) and clear.
• Touch & hold the date for 1.5 seconds = these settings.
